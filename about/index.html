About Keelworks

Hands-on cloud and infrastructure experience, built into a practical technology services company.

Who we are

Technology services built around real engineering experience.

Keelworks is a founder-led technology services company helping startups and growing businesses build, run and improve their cloud and IT infrastructure.

The company was started by Naveen, a hands-on technology professional working across cloud architecture, DevOps, SRE, platform engineering and infrastructure operations.

The focus is simple: solve the infrastructure problem that a business actually has, without adding unnecessary tools, complexity or cost.

Why Keelworks

Built for companies that are growing faster than their infrastructure team.

Many companies reach a point where their applications and cloud infrastructure have become too important to manage informally, but they are not yet ready to build a large internal platform or infrastructure team.

Hands-on engineering

Cloud architecture, infrastructure automation, CI/CD, containers, monitoring and operational improvements based on practical engineering experience.

Operational thinking

Infrastructure is not finished when it is deployed. We also consider monitoring, backups, patching, security, reliability and ongoing support.

Business-aware decisions

Recommendations consider cost, team capability, operational effort and business priorities, not just technical possibilities.

Direct involvement

Keelworks is founder-led, which means clients can work directly with the person responsible for understanding the technical problem and delivery.

What we do

One company, from infrastructure to people.

Keelworks brings together several areas that are often handled by separate vendors or teams.

  • Cloud & DevOps
    Build and improve AWS, Azure and GCP environments, infrastructure automation, CI/CD, containers and platform capabilities.
  • Managed IT & Cloud Operations
    Handle ongoing infrastructure operations including monitoring, backups, patching, security maintenance and incident support.
  • Migration & Modernization
    Move applications and infrastructure to the cloud or between environments and modernize systems that have outgrown their current setup.
  • Technical Recruitment
    Help companies find Cloud, DevOps, SRE, Platform, Infrastructure and DevSecOps engineers.
  • Cloud & DevOps Training
    Provide practical technical training and mentoring for engineers and teams working with modern cloud infrastructure.
How we think

Keep infrastructure useful, understandable and maintainable.

Use the right level of technology

Not every company needs Kubernetes, multi-cloud or a large platform engineering stack. Technology should match the actual requirement.

Automate repetitive work

Infrastructure and operational work should be repeatable wherever possible, using automation and infrastructure as code.

Think about operations early

Monitoring, backups, patching, security and incident handling should be considered when systems are designed, not after something goes wrong.

Build for the team you have

A technically impressive environment is not useful if the team cannot operate it. Solutions should be practical for the people who will maintain them.

The goal

Help companies grow without letting infrastructure become a bottleneck.

Keelworks can start with a focused project, provide ongoing infrastructure support, help with a migration, or help build the internal team that eventually takes ownership.

The engagement can grow or reduce as the business changes. The objective is not to keep a client dependent on an external provider. It is to make the technology environment easier to run and improve.
