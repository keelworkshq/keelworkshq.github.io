About

I'm Naveen, and Keelworks is me, working directly with you, not a bigger team behind the scenes.

I work across cloud architecture, DevOps, SRE and platform engineering, depending on what a project actually needs. I build and ship software myself too, so infrastructure decisions are grounded in how applications actually get built and run, not just how they're hosted.

I started Keelworks because I kept seeing the same situation: a growing company running real production workloads on AWS, Azure or GCP, without the headcount yet to justify hiring a dedicated platform or DevOps engineer. The infrastructure still needs to be looked after properly, and designed right from the start. It doesn't always need a full-time hire to do that.

That's what I do: architecture and hands-on DevOps work, migrations, day-to-day operations, or helping you hire the engineer who'll eventually take it over internally. Pay for what you actually need, and scale it up as your team grows.
