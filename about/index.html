About

I'm Naveen, and Keelworks is me, working directly with you, not a bigger team behind the scenes.

I work in cloud and DevOps, and started Keelworks because I kept seeing the same situation: a startup or SME running real production workloads on AWS or Azure, without the headcount yet to justify hiring a dedicated platform or DevOps engineer. The infrastructure still needs to be looked after properly. It doesn't need a full-time hire to do that, at least not yet.

That's what I do: hands-on CloudOps and DevOps work, day-to-day IT support, or helping you hire the engineer who'll eventually take it over internally. Pay for what you actually need, and we can scale it up as your team grows.
