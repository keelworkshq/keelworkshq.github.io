Cloud & DevOps Recruitment

Technical recruitment with engineering-led screening for Cloud, DevOps, SRE, Platform and DevSecOps roles.

Why Keelworks

Technical roles need more than keyword matching

Hiring for infrastructure and cloud roles can be difficult when a CV lists dozens of technologies but does not show how the person has actually used them. Keelworks focuses on the technical experience behind the CV before presenting candidates to you.

  • Understand the role
    We first understand your infrastructure, technology stack, team structure and what the person will actually be responsible for.
  • Technical screening
    Candidates are reviewed for relevant hands-on experience across the technologies and responsibilities required for the role.
  • Relevant candidates
    We focus on candidates whose experience matches the actual role rather than sending large volumes of loosely matched CVs.
  • Clear candidate information
    Candidate profiles include relevant experience, technical strengths and areas that should be explored during your interview process.

How it works

From role definition to offer

1. Understand

We discuss the role, team, technology stack, responsibilities and experience required.

2. Source

We identify candidates based on the agreed technical and professional requirements.

3. Screen

Candidates are reviewed for relevant technical experience before being presented to your team.

4. Interview

You interview shortlisted candidates using your own technical and cultural assessment process.

5. Offer & join

We coordinate with both sides through the offer and joining process.
