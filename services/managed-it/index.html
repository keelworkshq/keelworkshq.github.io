Managed IT & Cloud Operations

Keep your servers, cloud infrastructure, backups and monitoring under control without having to build a dedicated infrastructure operations team.

What we do

Ongoing infrastructure operations, handled by us

Keelworks provides ongoing operational support for companies that have cloud or server infrastructure but do not yet need, or cannot justify, a full-time infrastructure team.

  • Server and cloud management
    Day-to-day management of servers, virtual machines, cloud resources and supporting infrastructure.
  • Monitoring and alerting
    Monitor infrastructure, services and key resources so operational issues can be identified before they become larger problems.
  • Backup management
    Backup monitoring, configuration checks and regular validation of your existing backup processes.
  • Patch management
    Operating system and infrastructure patching with planned maintenance and environment-wise rollout.
  • Security maintenance
    Practical infrastructure hardening, access reviews and ongoing security improvements.
  • Incident support
    Investigation and resolution support when infrastructure or operational issues affect your applications.
  • Infrastructure maintenance
    Routine configuration changes, housekeeping, capacity checks and other operational tasks.
  • Monthly reporting
    Regular reporting on infrastructure health, incidents, backups, patching and other agreed operational areas.
Who this is for

A practical option for growing teams

This service is designed for startups and growing companies that have infrastructure to operate but do not want to build a large CloudOps or infrastructure operations team yet.

  • Companies running workloads on AWS, Azure or GCP
  • Teams managing a mix of cloud and virtual machines
  • Companies that need regular patching and backup management
  • Teams that need infrastructure monitoring and incident support
  • Companies that need additional infrastructure support alongside their engineering team
