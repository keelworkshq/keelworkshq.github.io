Set up properly

Everything around the website, done right

A website is more than its pages. We take care of the technical setup that many small websites miss, so your site and email work reliably from day one.

  • Domain, business email and DNS
    Help choosing and registering your domain, a professional email address such as you@yourcompany.com, and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records that keep your emails out of spam.
  • Hosting, SSL, speed and security
    Reliable hosting, HTTPS on every page, fast loading on mobile networks and sensible security settings from the start.
  • Search basics (SEO)
    Clear page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, link previews for social media, and setup of Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and your Google Business Profile.
  • Enquiry features
    Contact forms with spam protection, a WhatsApp chat button and enquiries delivered straight to your business email.
