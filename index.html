A common problem

Your infrastructure has grown, but your team hasn't.

Many growing companies reach a point where their cloud and IT infrastructure has become too important to manage on an ad hoc basis, but they are not ready to hire a full infrastructure or platform team.

That's where Keelworks can help. We can work alongside your existing team or take care of specific parts of your infrastructure for you.

Where we can help

From setup to day-to-day operations

  • Cloud setup and architecture
    Plan and build AWS, Azure or GCP environments that fit your application and business needs.
  • Migration and modernization
    Move applications and infrastructure to the cloud, modernize existing environments and reduce unnecessary complexity.
  • DevOps and automation
    Improve deployment processes with CI/CD, Terraform, infrastructure automation and better release practices.
  • Monitoring and reliability
    Set up monitoring, alerting, backups and operational processes so problems can be identified and handled earlier.
  • Managed infrastructure

    Help find engineers with real experience in Cloud, DevOps, SRE and Platform Engineering.
